Location: Planetary orbit near Heaven's Gate
Date: 213.04.02

{Heaven's Gate} is located on the outskirts of {Valkyrian} systems, a natural wormhole in a metastable state.

In the early days of the sector, this short-range corridor served as an economic engine, fueling the expansion of the region's mining empires, but following the Collapse, famine and endless deflation shattered the corporate giants that had entrenched themselves here. Soon after, wandering {Valkyries} stepped in and gained control.

During military campaigns linked to the {Atlantis Nebula}, {Heaven's Gate} proved its strategic worth. Opposition from other powers forced the {Valkyries} to maintain a massive fleet, onr capable of securing the wormhole while remaining a force to be reckoned with regards to external conflicts. The planetary orbital base fleet under the regional governor of the {Heaven's Gate} and the Royal Fleet guarding the Enigma system accounted for 60% of military expenditures.

...

As times changed and the modernization of the antiquated military strained national reserves, this fragile balance collapsed when a rebellion triggered a financial crisis that intensified tensions between the military, government, and civilian population, culminating in a full-scale civil War.

Troops stationed in various places were recalled one after another, and the space marshals of the {Hegemony}, keenly aware of the gaps in the {Valkyries'} border defenses, deployed a large number of regular and mercenary fleets to challenge the {Valkyries} from multiple vectors. Though most attacks were repulsed within three days, a {Hegemony} war fleet that was hidden within the barrage of full-frontal assaults struck directly at {Heaven's Gate}.

The new queen's flagship, fresh from civil war, was still sailing through hyperspace when she ordered a retreat, demanding that the governor of {Heaven's Gate} abandon the stronghold and to join the decisive battle planned by the crown.

The governor firmly refused, and choose to uphold both the glory of the {Valkyrie} and {Heaven's Gate's} ancient right to be independent of the crown. Although the ambitions of the Hegemony were thwarted, the governor fell in battle, and the Heaven's Gate region, bereft of most of its military power, was eventually swept up by the end of the modernization era. Royal Edict #12: The Heaven's Gate governor's district is thereby dissolved, along with any local tax privileges, economic entitlements and self-governing rights, and the Second Royal Guard and the Planetary Defense Fleet will thereafter be responsible for the defense of the region.
