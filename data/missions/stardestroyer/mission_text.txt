Location: ES-19J
Date: 208.07.22

After the destruction of the alien mothership, the Valkyries had gone eerily quiet. The assembly lines at their docks lay dormant, with large numbers of workers and equipment redirected to logistics, with their transport destination yet unclear.

The space marshal overseeing the region on the {Hegemony} side frowned, realizing that things were not as simple as they seemed.

Indeed, things were not as they seemed.

Noticing the {Valkyrie's} unusual behavior, the marshal intensified his reconnaissance efforts. Despite the fact that the forces under his jurisdiction were in a passive defense phase after several rounds of significant drawdowns, the reconnaissance and probing continued at wartime frequency.

Several months later, a small {Hegemony} strike force detects an colossal super battleship in the debris field. Various observations indicated that construction was nearing completion.

The {Hegemony} immediately mobilizes every major combat ship remaining on the front lines against the Valkyrie for an annihilation mission. However, when they arrive, they were meet a "warm welcome" by the leviathan and her support fleet.

It looks like fire straight from the gates of hell.
