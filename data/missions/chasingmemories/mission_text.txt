Location: In an Unknown Nebula
Date: 174.03.26

Kanako, a strikingly beautiful captain was dismissed and sentenced to life imprisonment because she grew disillusioned with the cruelty of the Valkyries.

The female captain, born a foot soldier, quickly escaped from prison and fled to the shuttle port. There, she hid in a container loaded on a tractor-trailer bound for the Naval Assembly Plant.

As luck would have it, she found an Nirvana Mk.II class on the production line and fled the Valkyrian space station.

Her breakout did not escape the eyes of patrols, and several patrol ships on perimeter watch were immediately ordered to pursue her. The veteran captain skillfully switched her flagship to low-power drive and dove into a nebula to hide her sensor signature and engine trails.

However, the Jenova frigate, with its advanced sensors, still managed to lock onto her anomalous emissions......

Will she escape?
