Location: NW-3B
Date: 201.02.07

The newly developed {VKS} Valkyria {Yoshura} Mk.II class had just been launched, her maiden voyage destined for the frontline in the Ophiuchus system. However, after encountering what is suspected to be jump disruptor interference, Valkyria and her escort detachment were forced to exit hyperspace.

Unfortunately, they emerged right in the heart of a massive pirate fleet, with hundreds of ships flying red skull flags clustered around a pirate stronghold.

The escort fleet's commander issued clear orders: half the warships were to form a circular line to repel the pirate ships, and the rest were to evacuate with the escort target. But the captain of VKS {Valkyria}, driven by a sense of heroism, instead ordered the flagship to engage in tactical maneuvers at full engine thrust, rather than switch to long-distance travel mode.

The VKS {Valkyria} surged forward, breaking away from the retreat formation. The commander was forced to terminate sustained burn and order his ships to maintain sufficient distance to intervene in the battle.

The entire retreat fleet turned and charged toward the pirate fleet's left flank.

They have no choice but to defend with all that they have.
