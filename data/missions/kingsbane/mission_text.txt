Location: Deep Space
Date: 207.05.07

A battle broke out between the Valkyrie and Blackrock Drive Yards when the former could not longer tolerate their lessers sneaking around their high-tech mining fleet and copying their technology.

VKS Exile, a Valkyrie super battleship, sat at the rear of the formation, bolstering morale and providing fleet-wide defensive coverage.

However, Valkyrie high command were unaware that the other side had hired mercenaries to target this giant ship. Once VKS Exile was out the reach of their main force, this mercenary fleet would ambush and clip the wings of the Valkyrie.

VKS Exile is in good condition, and while not at full strength, her accompanying escort fleet packs strong firepower. Both sides enter combat brimming with confidence.
