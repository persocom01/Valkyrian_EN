Location: SE-1A
Date: 237.06.22

You are ordered to take command of a medium Valkyrian carrier strike group to wipe out the enemy.

According to radar, the enemy fleet is vast, lead by 3 lumbering Onslaught-class battleships. For a moment, you panic, but upon further inspection, the enemy seems to have no carriers.

A faint smirk curls your lips, and you know just what you have to do.
